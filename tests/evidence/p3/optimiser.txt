Battery-Health-Aware DP Charge Scheduler Evidence Summary
=========================================================
Document Reference: NOTE.md §2.3, §3.3 (Innovation 1), §6.4
Algorithm: Forward Dynamic Programming over 15-min slots, S=101 SoC states, A=4 actions

1. Representative Fleet Scenario:
   - Arrival Time:          14:00 (Start of Peak Tariff Window $0.280/kWh)
   - Departure Time:        23:00 (Post-Peak Window)
   - Battery Initial / Target: 25.0% -> 85.0% (75 kWh pack, 0.95 SoH)
   - Baseline Cost:         $11.97 (Charge-on-arrival at full 100 kW during peak)
   - DP Optimized Cost:     $6.44 (Delayed charging to off-peak / valley slots)
   - Scenario Cost Savings: -46.20%
   - Scenario Stress Reduc: -88.04%
   - Single-Vehicle Runtime: 1.016 ms

2. Large-Scale Fleet Benchmark (1,000 Vehicles):
   - Total 1,000-Vehicle Execution Time: 1,908.8 ms (1.91 s) (~1.91 ms/vehicle)
   - Net Portfolio Baseline Cost:        $8,500.64
   - Net Portfolio DP Optimized Cost:    $5,815.99
   - Net Fleet Portfolio Cost Savings:   31.58% (Mean vehicle savings: 26.21%)
   - Mean Battery Degradation Reduction: 83.51%
   - Solution Feasibility Rate:          100.0% (1,000 / 1,000 vehicles)

3. Core Innovation Highlight (Solution Document §3.3):
   Traditional smart chargers optimize purely for time-of-use (TOU) tariff pricing, which
   often induces concentrated maximum-power spikes during the cheapest slot, accelerating
   lithium-ion battery degradation. Our DP scheduler formulates a dual-objective cost function:
   Cost = Tariff(slot) * kWh + lambda * (kW / max_kW)^2 * kWh
   This penalizes extreme C-rates, achieving over 83% reduction in battery stress while
   simultaneously capturing 31.58% net electricity savings.
